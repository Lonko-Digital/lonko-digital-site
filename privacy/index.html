Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy takes effect on August 22, 2026.

This Privacy Policy applies to the public Lonko Digital website (the “Site”). It does not apply to private internal systems, the private Lonko Digital Marketing Intelligence Platform, or any future commercial services that may publish separate policies.

Who we are

Lonko Digital operates this public informational website.

The Site is intended to provide company information and public policies only.

What this Site is today

The current public Site is informational and early-stage. As designed today, it:

  • Does not offer public customer accounts
  • Does not offer subscriptions
  • Does not accept payments
  • Does not provide public access to commercial software
  • Does not publicly connect Google Ads accounts
  • Does not publicly connect LinkedIn accounts
  • Does not ingest customer marketing-account data
  • Uses Google Tag Manager (container ID GTM-53DPJ88F) to manage the tags described below
  • Uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure site traffic and engagement, including standard page views and two custom events: link/button clicks and scroll depth
  • Uses a Google Ads tag, which supports Google Ads-related measurement and may support remarketing or audience-based advertising functionality made available by Google
  • Uses a LinkedIn Insight Tag, which supports LinkedIn advertising-related measurement and may support remarketing or audience-based advertising functionality made available by LinkedIn
  • Does not currently use a Meta Pixel or other third-party advertising pixels beyond those listed above
  • Does not include a server-side contact form

Information you may provide

If you choose to contact Lonko Digital by email at lonkodigital@gmail.com, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, and message content. That information is used only to respond to your inquiry and maintain ordinary business records related to the conversation.

Please do not send passwords, API keys, account credentials, OAuth tokens, marketing account identifiers, or other sensitive personal or security information through ordinary contact or email channels.

Information collected automatically

Hosting and server logs

The Site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and related infrastructure may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps, and referrer URLs in server or CDN logs for security, abuse prevention, and service operation.

Lonko Digital does not control all aspects of GitHub’s infrastructure logging. For details about GitHub’s processing, see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Local appearance preference

The Site may store a light/dark appearance preference in your browser’s local storage under the key lonko-public-appearance. This is used only to remember your display preference on return visits. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

Analytics and advertising tags

The Site loads Google Tag Manager container GTM-53DPJ88F, which manages the following tags:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): collects standard site-usage information such as pages viewed, and two custom events — when a visitor clicks a link or button, and how far a visitor scrolls down a page.
  • Google Ads tag: supports measurement related to Google Ads. This tag may enable Google to associate Site visits with advertising activity, including remarketing (showing ads to past visitors elsewhere) where Google's own advertising tools are used.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag: supports measurement related to LinkedIn advertising. This tag may enable LinkedIn to associate Site visits with advertising activity, including remarketing, where LinkedIn's own advertising tools are used.

These tags are provided and operated by Google and LinkedIn respectively. Lonko Digital does not directly control how Google or LinkedIn process the information these tags collect; their own privacy policies govern that processing:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • LinkedIn Privacy Policy

If additional tags are configured through this container in the future, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Cookies and similar technologies

The tags described above (GA4, Google Ads tag, LinkedIn Insight Tag) may set cookies or use similar technologies in your browser to support the measurement and advertising functions described. Depending on your browser settings and location, you may be able to limit or block these technologies — see “Your choices” below.

How we use information

Information associated with this Site is used only to:

  • Operate and secure the public Site
  • Respond to voluntary inquiries
  • Maintain and improve Site content
  • Comply with applicable law when required

We do not sell personal information through this Site.

Retention

Voluntary email correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain ordinary business records. Infrastructure logs are retained according to hosting-provider practices. Appearance preferences remain in your browser until you clear site data.

Security

Reasonable measures appropriate to a static public website are used. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Do not submit sensitive credentials or sensitive personal information through public contact channels.

External links

The Site may link to third-party websites for convenience. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information.

Children’s privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, or the applicable age in your jurisdiction. Lonko Digital does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this Site.

International visitors

Visitors may access the Site from various locations. Hosting infrastructure may process technical data in countries where GitHub or related providers operate.

Your choices

  • You may clear local storage in your browser to remove appearance preferences.
  • You may choose not to contact us by email.
  • You may stop using the Site at any time.
  • You may use your browser’s cookie controls, or tools such as Google’s Ads Settings and LinkedIn’s advertising preferences, to limit how Google and LinkedIn use information collected through this Site for advertising purposes.

Questions About Your Information

If you have questions about how we handle information you've provided to us, please contact us at lonkodigital@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Site changes. The effective date will be revised and material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

This policy covers the current informational public website only. Future commercial services, customer accounts, OAuth connections, payments, or customer marketing-data processing would require separate policies and appropriate legal review.
